About this illustrated load and its measurements

The compact Luton load is an illustration using the internal bay dimensions of a Ford Transit L3 dual rear wheel Luton from Ford’s published specification. Item selection uses an approximate volume allowance for planning, not a guaranteed packing plan. Actual fit depends on the vehicle, access, packing and payload; Enterprise’s van guide gives an example Luton payload. Object dimensions are based on example IKEA products: box, sofa, chest, shelf, wardrobe, bed frame and bedside drawers.
